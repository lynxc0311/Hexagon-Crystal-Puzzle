Hexagon Crystal Puzzle

HOW TO PLAY

Left click: Rotate the three crystals 120° clockwise.

Right click or Shift + left click: Rotate 120° counterclockwise.

Double left click: Flip the three crystals over in 3D.

Touch controls: Tap to rotate clockwise, press and hold to rotate counterclockwise, or double-tap to flip.

Hint: Highlights the next pivot and displays the move to make. Repeat to solve the puzzle.

Undo reverses your last move. Play scrambles the puzzle using reversible moves, so every scramble is solvable.
